Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what data Playloggr collects, what we use it for, and what rights you have over it. By creating an account or using the app (web or Android), you accept this policy.

1. Data we collect

DataWhat we use it for
Email and password (hashed)Creating your account and identifying you when you log in
Username, bio, avatarYour public profile within the app (if you make it public)
Game library, ratings, hours playedThe core functioning of your backlog
Steam ID and your Steam library dataSyncing your library and wishlist if you connect your account (optional, premium plan)
Friendships and requestsThe social feature of adding friends and seeing their activity
Achievements, XP, and levelThe gamification system
Push notification token (FCM)Sending you friend request notifications (Android app only)
IP address and technical device dataError diagnostics (Sentry) and security
Analytics cookies (Google Analytics)Understanding site usage — only if you accept the cookie banner

2. Third-party services we use

  • Railway — backend and database hosting.
  • Google Sign-In — optional login with your Google account.
  • Steam Web API — library/wishlist sync if you link your Steam account (optional).
  • Resend — sending the password recovery email.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications on the Android app.
  • Sentry — technical error monitoring for the backend.
  • Google Analytics — website usage statistics, only if you accept cookies.

None of these services receive your password. We don't sell your data to third parties or use it for our own advertising purposes.

3. How long we keep your data

For as long as your account exists. You can delete your library or your entire account (with all associated data) at any time from Settings → Account, on both web and app.

4. Your rights

  • Access the data we have about you (your profile, library, and statistics are already visible within the app).
  • Correct your information from your profile.
  • Permanently delete your account and all your data.
  • Reject analytics cookies without affecting how the app works.

5. Public profile

By default your profile is public (visible to other users so they can add you as a friend). You can make it private from Settings at any time — that way only your confirmed friends can see your library and activity.

6. Minors

Playloggr is not directed at children under 13. If we detect an account belonging to a minor under that age, we delete it.

7. Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll reflect them here with a new update date.
