Stop forgetting what you were playing.

Features

Everything you need to keep track of your backlog

From simple game tracking to advanced statistics and gamification.

📚

Library and wishlist

Organize your games by status (playing, completed, backlog, dropped) and keep a separate wishlist.

🔄

Steam sync

Connect your Steam account and automatically sync your library, your wishlist, and community reviews.

🏆

Achievements and levels

150 achievements to unlock, an XP system, levels, avatar frames, and exclusive fonts as you progress.

📊

Real statistics

Hours played, ratings, breakdown by platform and genre, recent activity, and global rankings.

👥

Social backlog

Add friends, see what they're playing in real time, and compare libraries.

🗂️

Custom folders

Group your games however you like: by series, by platform, by priority — whatever makes sense to you.
