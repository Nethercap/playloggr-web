    Questions we get a lot

    Is it safe to connect my accounts?

    We never ask for your password. Steam and PlayStation work with your public ID, and GOG, Xbox and Epic with each platform’s official login: we only get permission to read your library.

    Is it a subscription?

    No. Playloggr is free and Premium is a one-time payment of USD 6.99, with no auto-renewal.

    What does the free plan include?

    Unlimited library and wishlist, sync with all 5 platforms, all 145 achievements, XP and every level, stats, reviews and your public profile.

    Is there an iPhone app?

    For now the app is for Android. On iPhone you can use the web version at playloggr.com/app, with the same account and the same features.

    Does it work with Nintendo Switch?

    Nintendo doesn’t offer a way to read your library, so Switch games are added by hand (with cover art) and you can track hours with the session timer.

    Can I use it on my computer and my phone?

    Yes. The web and the Android app share the same account and everything syncs on its own.
