Features

Everything you need to keep track of your backlog

From simple game tracking to advanced statistics and gamification.

📚

Library and wishlist

  • Statuses: playing, completed, backlog, dropped, on hold, plus a separate wishlist
  • Filters by status, platform, hours, and rating
  • List or grid view, with a compact mode
  • Custom folders by series, platform, or priority
  • Live play session with a timer
🔄

Platform sync

  • Steam: library, wishlist, and community reviews
  • PlayStation: PS4, PS5, PS3, and Vita, with playtime and cover art
  • GOG, Xbox, and Epic Games: automatic library with cover art
  • Daily automatic sync, no need to open the app
🏆

Achievements and levels Premium

139 achievements to unlock, plus an XP and leveling system as you progress.

📊

Statistics and rankings

  • Hours played, ratings, and breakdown by platform
  • Activity heatmap by day (year, month, or week)
  • Community-wide global rankings
  • Your own custom rankings — top 10 RPGs, favorites of the year, whatever you want
👥

Social backlog

Add friends, see what they're playing in real time, and compare libraries.

🧑‍🎨

Customizable avatar Premium

Build your pixel-art avatar with outfits, frames, and exclusive fonts you unlock as you level up.

🗺️

Game plan Premium

Chain games together with duration estimates from HowLongToBeat, set how many hours you play per week, and track your progress week by week until you finish them.

🎨

Premium themes Premium

Themes and interface inspired by iconic video games.

🗳️

Game of the month Premium

Vote for your pick between the 2nd and 9th of each month and check the result and the history of past months.

🔒

We never ask for your password

For Steam and PlayStation we only ask for your public ID (SteamID/Online ID) — you don't even need to log in. For GOG, Xbox and Epic Games the login always happens on that platform's own official page (OAuth login): we never see or receive your password, only a permission to read your library. We never share your data with third parties, in either case.
